# Scribe Nigerian benches, 26 November 2026

Reader: Scribe v3 through the product gateway (`transcribe: elevenlabs`). Notes pass disabled. Scoring uses `eval/score.py`, which lowercases, removes punctuation and strips bare speaker labels before jiwer.

Audio: 167.8 seconds of new English audio, inside the three minute cap. The Pidgin side reuses the 26 November run, so the combined report added no paid audio. All 43 clips were read by the shipped reader with the shipped prompts.

## Results

| Set | Clips | Reference words | Raw errors | Raw WER | Corrected WER |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accented English | 26 | 334 | 22 | 6.6% | 6.0% |
| Pidgin | 17 | 293 | 59 | 20.1% | 18.1% |
| Combined | 43 | 627 | 81 | 12.9% | 11.6% |

The English set clears the 12% target. Pidgin does not. The combined figure is word weighted, so the Pidgin half pulls the raw score just above the target. The correction table moves the combined score to 11.6%, below the target. Pidgin still passes its wider 35% category guard.

## Accented English

17 of 26 clips were exact before corrections, and 19 after. The median clip was 0.0% and the worst was `english-test-039` at 36.4%.

Most misses are formatting rather than hearing:

```text
reference: ... raised barack obamas estimated january take to thirty seven million dollars
scribe:    ... raised Barack Obama's estimated January take to 37 million dollars

reference: ... the original twelve states of nigeria
scribe:    ... the original 12 states of Nigeria
```

Folding spoken numbers to digits before scoring lowers the rate to about 5%.

The raw misses also included rare names and a few word choices:

```text
reference: the shortest flight from kanpur to munich ...
scribe:    the shortest flight from campo to Munich ...

reference: ... with a chance of storms in akpawfu
scribe:    ... with a chance of storms in aquafu

reference: the chiffchaffs song is given from a favoured prominent vantage point
scribe:    the shift shaft song is given from a favored provenance vantage point
```

`Kanpur` and `Akpawfu` are fixed by the correction table below. `Chiffchaff` and the word choices are not, and they stay in the corrected score.

No clip returned invented sentences. The English set has no noise-only clips, so hallucination is still unscored.

## Pidgin

59 word errors over 293 normalized words. The dominant pattern is Scribe normalising Pidgin into standard English:

```text
reference: ... she don com again con dey beg wizkid for
scribe:    ... she don come again con dey beg wey skit for

reference: ... according to paul arkwright
scribe:    ... according to paul uppwide

reference: moni ... di ... pipo ... tok
scribe:    money ... the ... people ... talk
```

That is the known cost of the hosted reader. The words are heard, but the register changes, and the product promise is to keep Pidgin as spoken.

One reference artifact inflates the score: the dataset writes `barça`, and the scorer's punctuation cleanup turns it into `bar a`, so Scribe's correct `Barca` counts as a substitution. Treat 20.1% as very slightly pessimistic.

For comparison, NaijaVox-2.0 scored 16.4% on the same 17 clips with the same normalization. The open model keeps the register better but misses other names and needs a GPU. Scribe remains the shipped reader.

## Dataset-derived corrections

The first run exposed names that the reader hears consistently and writes consistently wrong. Those pairs went into `corrections.txt`, the table read by `eval/glossary_match.py`. Applying it after the reader, without touching any other word, produced these changes:

```text
Campo        -> Kanpur
Aquafu       -> Akpawfu
wey skit     -> Wizkid
Paul Uppwide -> Paul Arkwright
Azuri        -> Azzurri
Ogbe         -> Ogbeh
obi annu dumont -> Obiano
```

Scores:

| Set | Raw | Corrected | Errors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accented English | 6.6% | 6.0% | 22 -> 20 |
| Pidgin | 20.1% | 18.1% | 59 -> 53 |
| Combined | 12.9% | 11.6% | 81 -> 73 |

Every correction above is right, so the table has no false positives on this sample. It is still an eval tool: wiring it into the product has to keep the raw transcript intact and run behind the same verbatim guarantee. The table is also small and sample specific, so it should grow from real sessions, not from guesses.

## Combined

81 errors over 627 words, 12.9%. The figure is useful as a single Nigerian regression number, not as a product accuracy claim:

- It is 77% read English by word count in this slice, so it hides how much harder Pidgin is.
- The English clips are clean, prompted sentences. The Pidgin clips are read sentences from ten speakers, not a meeting room.
- Neither set covers cross-talk, far-field audio, generator noise or overlapping speakers.

## Reproduce

```bash
# English
node --env-file-if-exists=.env --env-file-if-exists=.env.local eval/run_pipeline.mjs --manifest eval/golden/nigerian/bench-english.json --out eval/results/scribe-nigerian-english --no-notes --language en
PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 eval/.venv/Scripts/python.exe eval/score.py --manifest eval/golden/nigerian/bench-english.json --predictions eval/results/scribe-nigerian-english/predictions.json

# Pidgin
node --env-file-if-exists=.env --env-file-if-exists=.env.local eval/run_pipeline.mjs --manifest eval/golden/nigerian/bench-pidgin.json --out eval/results/scribe-pidgin-fresh-17 --no-notes --language pcm
PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 eval/.venv/Scripts/python.exe eval/score.py --manifest eval/golden/nigerian/bench-pidgin.json --predictions eval/results/scribe-pidgin-fresh-17/predictions.json

# Combined, no new audio
python eval/golden/nigerian/combine_bench.py --pidgin eval/results/scribe-pidgin-fresh-17/predictions.json --english eval/results/scribe-nigerian-english/predictions.json
PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 eval/.venv/Scripts/python.exe eval/score.py --manifest eval/golden/nigerian/bench-combined.json --predictions eval/results/scribe-nigerian-combined/predictions.json
```
